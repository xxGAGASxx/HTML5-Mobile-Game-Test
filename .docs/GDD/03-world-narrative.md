# 03. World & Narrative

## Reference [O/R]

- An explorers' ship crashed on a mysterious island [O].
- The player is a survivor leading an expedition to find other survivors and regain strength [O].
- Ancient ruins, dungeons and powerful artifacts hide the island's secrets [O].
- Enemies include humans, monsters and skeletons [R]. Biomes include beach, deep forest,
  dungeon and desert [R].
- Story unfolds gradually through combat and has an ending players reach [R].
- Medieval fantasy tone with knights and magic [R].

## Our world [P]

### Premise

The *Gilded Gull*, a bounty-hunter guild ship, wrecks on **Vael**, an island that appears on no
chart. The Captain (player avatar) wakes on the beach with a handful of crew. Rival wreck
survivors, bandits, beasts and the restless dead all want what little there is. A drowned empire's
ruins hold relics that bend fate, and something below the island keeps calling ships to it.

### Tone

Light-hearted adventure with a dark undertone; banter between units; violence is cartoonish
(12+ moderate violence is acceptable, no gore).

### Factions

| Faction | Role | Persuadable? |
|---|---|---|
| Gull Crew | Starting allies | n/a |
| Castaways | Other wreck survivors, rescued via story | Recruited by quest |
| Reef Bandits | Human enemies, region 1–2 | Yes |
| Wildkin | Beasts and beast-folk of the forest | Yes (some) |
| Sand Cult | Desert zealots | Yes, rare |
| Hollow Legion | Skeletons and undead of the ruins | Only with a relic |
| The Tide Below | Final antagonist | No |

### Regions (story arc)

1. **Wreck Coast** (beach): tutorial, bandits, crabs. Boss: Bandit Chief.
2. **Whisperwood** (deep forest): beasts, druids. Boss: Elder Stag.
3. **Sunken Steps** (dungeon/ruins): skeletons. Boss: Ossuary Knight.
4. **Glass Dunes** (desert): cultists, scorpions. Boss: Sun Prophet.
5. **Drowned Crown** (sea caverns): final arc. Boss: The Tide Below.

### Story delivery

- Short portrait dialogue cards (2–4 lines) before and after key encounters.
- Found journal pages in ruins, collectible in a Codex.
- Persuaded units get a one-line join quote; this costs little and makes the roster feel alive.
- Post-story: Deep Ruins endless mode plus weekly "Bounty Contracts" with light narrative framing,
  so content does not stop when the story does (a reported pain point [R]).
