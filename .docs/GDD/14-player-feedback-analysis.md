# 14. Player Feedback Analysis

Based on a small public sample (Play Store reviews shown on the page, plus comments mirrored on
third-party sites). The sample is small and skews toward people who leave text reviews; weigh accordingly.

## What players like [R]

| Signal | Evidence | Keep in our game |
|---|---|---|
| Addictive progression | "incredible, really addictive"; 4.6 average | Short loops, frequent rewards |
| A story with an end | "finished the storyline, eager for more" | Story arc plus post-story content |
| Knight and magic animation | Positive comment | Readable, juicy unit animation |
| Offline play | Store tag and description | Full offline support |

## What players dislike [R]

| Pain point | Evidence | Our response | Doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mid-game wall | "hard stick in the middle" | Catch-up bonus, goal bar, loosening economy | 07, 08 |
| Slow saving to hire | "a week to save up to hire your army" | Hiring targets: 25 min first army, 3 days full | 06 |
| Skill costs out of scale | "4k when you only get 50–100" | Max cost = 4 h of income | 07, 08 |
| Mindless clicking | "brainless clicker", "constant clicking tiresome" | Taps charge a meaningful skill meter | 05 |
| Long, repetitive battles | "battles drag", "long and repetitive" | 45 s cap, 2x/3x speed, auto-clear trivial | 04, 05 |
| Consumables to fight | "even fair battles require limited consumables" | No mandatory consumables | 05 |
| Slow respawns | "easy mobs 6 h respawn" | 2 h respawns, bounty board | 04 |
| Weak ads | "3 daily ads with little benefit" | Each ad worth about 20 min of income | 10 |
| Big numbers | "ridiculous HP and damage" | Gentle growth, short format | 05 |
| No explanation | "lack of explanation" | Contextual tutorials | 11 |
| Content ran out, no dev comms | "no reply about new content" | Endless Deep Ruins, roadmap in game | 03, 15 |
| No upgrade preview | Third-party review | Before/after stats | 07, 11 |
