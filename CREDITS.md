# Credits

## Pixel art

Units, effects and battlefield tiles are self-made, generated from `art/pixel/` by `npm run sprites`.

## Icons

UI icons come from [game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), licensed under
[CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The files in `public/icons/` were taken
from the [game-icons/icons](https://github.com/game-icons/icons) repository. They were changed:
the black background square was removed so the white silhouettes can be tinted in game.

| Icon | Author |
|---|---|
| meat, crossed-swords, sword-clash | Lorc |
| two-coins, camping-tent, fast-forward-button | Delapouite |

The camp screen shows a short attribution line; a full Credits screen comes with Settings (GDD 11).
